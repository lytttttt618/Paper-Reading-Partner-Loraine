Open a PDF paper to start reading. 打开一篇论文开始阅读。

or drag & drop a PDF file here

  • Line focus follows your mouse and greys out the rest. Click the paper to switch it on/off; ↑ / ↓ move line by line.
  • Double-click a word or select text → Translate · Ask AI · Highlight · Note.
  • Notes support Markdown and LaTeX: $E=mc^2$, $$\int_0^1 x\,dx$$.
  • Everything is saved automatically in this browser.
